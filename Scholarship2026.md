## 2. In-Course & Research Funding (For Current Enrolled 1st-Year Students)

If you are an enrolled first-year international student as of September 2026:

### A. UREX Summer Research Awards (Formerly PURE)
* **Value:** Up to **$7,500** for 8, 12, or 16 weeks of full-time summer research.
* **Eligibility:** Fully open to international undergraduate students who maintain good academic standing (GPA 3.2+ recommended).
* **Process:** Connect with a Biological Sciences faculty supervisor in October–December to co-author a research proposal.
* **Application Deadline:** Typically **late January / early February 2027**.

### B. Continuing Undergraduate Awards (Department of Biological Sciences)
Most department-specific biology endowments are awarded starting in Year 2 based on your UCalgary GPA:
* **Graeme Bell and Norma Kay Sullivan-Bell Undergraduate Scholarship in Biology**
* **Canadian Federation of University Women (CFUW) Calgary B. Lindley Scholarship** (3rd/4th year biology majors)
* **Grace LeBel Bursary** (Support for biological field station courses, such as Kananaskis)
* **Ninh Phu Huynh and Lanh Thi Lam Bursary**
* **Application Window:** Opens in the winter term via My UCalgary; annual deadline is **May 1, 2027**.

### C. Emergency & Need-Based Support
* **International Student Financial Need Bursary:** Administered through the Office of the Registrar via the Student Centre for registered international students facing unexpected financial changes or living cost pressures.

## 3. Graduate Funding (MSc / PhD in Biological Sciences)

For international students entering research-based graduate degrees (MSc / PhD) in Biological Sciences:

### Guaranteed Minimum Annual Stipend Policy
Under university regulations for thesis-based graduate students:
* **International PhD Students:** Guaranteed minimum **$33,205 CAD / year** (guaranteed for 4 years).
* **International MSc (Thesis) Students:** Guaranteed minimum **$25,455 CAD / year** (guaranteed for 2 years).

#### Package Components:
* **Supervisor Research Grants (Trust funds)**
* **Graduate Assistantships – Teaching (GAT):** Laboratory demonstrating/marking.
* **Graduate Assistantships – Non-Teaching (GANT / GRA):** Specific research assignments.
* **Departmental Graduate Research Scholarships (GRS):** Disbursed by the Department of Biological Sciences.

### Central Graduate Competitions:
* **Graduate Award Competition (GAC):** Central annual application for institutional awards (Killam Pre-Doctoral, Faculty of Graduate Studies scholarships). Deadline is typically **January 15**.
* **Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships:** $50,000/year for 3 years (PhD applicants).

## 4. Key Institutional & Canadian Immigration Policies (2026 Updates)

1. **Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) Requirement:**
   * International applicants requiring an initial Canadian Study Permit must receive a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) issued by Alberta through UCalgary.
   * UCalgary allocates PALs after you formally accept your admission offer and pay your admission deposit.
2. **Guaranteed International Tuition Framework:**
   * UCalgary locks in your international tuition rate for the typical duration of your undergraduate degree (4 years for a standard BSc), protecting you from unannounced annual percentage hikes.
3. **Off-Campus Work Regulations:**
   * Under Canadian immigration guidelines, full-time international students on a valid study permit may work off-campus up to **24 hours per week** during regular academic sessions and full-time during scheduled academic breaks.

### Practical Advice to Maximize Success:
* **Strive for a 95%+ Average:** Guarantees top consideration for the Faculty of Science Dean's Award and makes your Prestige application highly competitive.
* **Demonstrate Extracurricular Leadership:** Prestige award selection panels prioritize community involvement, science outreach, Olympiads, and collaborative leadership over raw grades alone.
* **Submit English Scores Early:** Submit IELTS (min 6.5, bands ≥ 6.0), TOEFL (min 86), or Duolingo (min 125) well in advance of the December 15 document deadline.
