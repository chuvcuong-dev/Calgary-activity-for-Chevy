## Essential Student Resource Hubs

1. **Biology Students Association (BSA):**
   * **Office:** Science Theatres (ST) 032
   * **Services:** BIO 241 & BIO 243 exam review sessions, lab coat sales, dissection kits, biology mixers.
   * **Website/Socials:** bsaucalgary.ca / Instagram: @bsaucalgary
2. **Faculty of Science Undergraduate Student Centre (USC):**
   * **Location:** Science B (SB) 149
   * **Services:** Degree planning, major requirements, science advising, Science Internship Program (SIP).
3. **Student Success Centre (SSC):**
   * **Location:** Taylor Family Digital Library (TFDL) 3rd Floor
   * **Services:** Peer-assisted study sessions (PASS), lab report writing support, study strategy coaching.
4. **International Student Services (ISS):**
   * **Location:** MacEwan Student Centre (MSC) 275
   * **Services:** Immigration advising (study permits/work authorization), Global Cafe, social orientation.
